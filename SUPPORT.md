# Support

Use the narrowest public channel that fits the request:

- Reproduction or software failure: open the **Bug or reproducibility failure** issue form.
- New experiment, data source, language rule, or integration: open the **Research or data
  proposal** issue form.
- Security or credential concern: follow `SECURITY.md` and do not publish secrets or private data.
- Conduct concern: follow `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` and avoid posting identifying details publicly.

The project is community-maintained and does not promise response times, hosted-service uptime,
model-provider availability, or individual research consultation. Include an exact revision,
commands, environment, expected behavior, and the smallest non-private fixture that reproduces the
problem. The maintainers cannot diagnose private corpus rows or provider secrets submitted only as
screenshots.
