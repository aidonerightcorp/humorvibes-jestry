# Community code of conduct

HumorVibes welcomes careful disagreement, negative results, reproductions, corrections, and
contributions from people with different languages, cultures, identities, disciplines, and levels
of technical experience.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims, methods, code, and evidence without attacking people.
- Treat a person's stated identity, language expertise, consent, and lived audience response as
  information you do not get to overwrite with a model score or demographic proxy.
- Ask before sharing private conversations, study records, unpublished text, or identifying data.
- Explain licensing and provenance concerns plainly and give contributors a chance to correct
  good-faith mistakes.
- Keep sexual, stereotyped, hateful, harassing, or humiliating material out of examples unless it
  is strictly necessary for a documented safety study and handled under an approved protocol.
- Respect maintainers' evidence gates even when a more exciting claim would be easier to market.

Harassment, threats, stalking, doxxing, discriminatory abuse, sexual attention, deliberate
misgendering, and retaliation are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the
project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an ordinary disagreement, open a focused issue and keep personal information out of it. For
a sensitive conduct or safety concern, contact the repository owner through the
`aidonerightcorp` GitHub account rather than posting details publicly. Maintainers may edit or
remove content, restrict participation, lock a thread, or ban an account when needed to protect
participants and the research record. Reports will be handled as privately as the available
channel permits; conflicts of interest should be disclosed and routed to an uninvolved reviewer
when one is available.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, study coordination, and
public events where someone is representing HumorVibes.
